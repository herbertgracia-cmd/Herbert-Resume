# Fromm Institute voice

Status: Draft. Built from Herbert's resume and LinkedIn. Herbert should confirm or edit before relying on it.

## In one sentence
The Fromm Institute for Lifelong Learning at the University of San Francisco offers classes, programs, and community for older adults in San Francisco.

## Audience
Older adults (many 65+) who love learning, plus their families, donors, faculty, and community partners such as retirement homes. Many aren't comfortable with websites, so clarity matters more than cleverness.

## Sounds like
- **Warm:** welcoming, like a friendly front desk.
- **Curious:** excited about ideas, never dumbed down.
- **Respectful:** treats older adults as lifelong contributors.

## Never sounds like
- **Patronizing:** no "seniors" clichés, no "young at heart", no talking down.
- **Ageist:** never frames age as decline. Elderhood is a time of contribution.
- **Techy or salesy:** no jargon, no hype, no pressure tactics.

## Always
- Plain words and short sentences. One idea per paragraph.
- Spell out the essentials: what, when, where, cost, and how to sign up.
- Give a person to contact (phone or email) alongside any online step.
- Say "older adults", "members", "learners", or "students".

## Never
- Invent class dates, fees, speakers, or deadlines. Use `[DATE]` and ask.
- Use more than one call to action per piece.
- Rely on emoji or hashtags to carry meaning.

## Facts we can use
- Part of the University of San Francisco.
- Serves 1,500+ older adult learners each year.
- Frommcast: Fromm's online learning and content platform; recorded classes are moving to Fromm's YouTube programs.
- Hybrid classes for in-person and online members.
- Celebrating its 50th Anniversary.
