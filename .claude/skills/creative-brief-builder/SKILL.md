# Creative brief builder

A good brief fits on one page and lets a designer, writer, or agency start work without a meeting. Build it from what Herbert gives you, and ask for what's missing instead of guessing.

## Steps

1. **Read everything Herbert gave you**: notes, emails, past briefs, links. If a brand voice profile exists in `brand-voices/`, read it for tone and audience.
2. **Fill what you can** in the template below, using Herbert's words where possible.
3. **Ask once, in one list**, for the missing essentials. Ask at most 6 questions, most important first. Never invent budgets, dates, metrics, names, or approvals; leave `[TBD]` instead.
4. **Sharpen the core.** The *single-minded message* must be one sentence a stranger would remember. Offer 2–3 options if it's unclear, and recommend one.
5. **Pressure-test** with the checks at the end, then fix anything that fails.
6. **Hand it over** in Markdown. If Herbert asks, save it to `briefs/<yyyy-mm-dd>-<project>.md`.

## Template

```markdown
# [Project name], creative brief
Owner: Herbert Gracia · Date: [today] · Status: Draft / Approved

## Background
2–3 sentences: what's happening and why now.

## Objective
What success looks like, as a measurable result (for example "500 sign-ups by [date]"). If there's no number yet, say [TBD].

## Audience
Who, in one line. What they believe now, and what we want them to believe or do after.

## Insight
The human truth that makes this work (one sentence).

## Single-minded message
One sentence.

## Reasons to believe
3 proof points (facts only).

## Tone
3 words, plus a link to the brand voice profile if there is one.

## Deliverables
| Item | Channel / format | Size / length | Due |
|---|---|---|---|

## Mandatories
Logos, legal lines, accessibility needs, partner credits, things that must appear.

## Budget & resources
[TBD unless given]

## Timeline
Milestones: brief approved → first concepts → review → final → launch.

## Success metrics
How and when we'll measure.

## Approvals
Who signs off at each stage.

## References
Links, past work, moodboard ideas.
```

## Checks before handing over

- Could a designer start tomorrow with only this page?
- Is the objective measurable (or clearly marked [TBD])?
- Is there exactly one single-minded message?
- Does every deliverable have a format and a due date (or [TBD])?
- Is every fact real? No invented numbers, names, or dates.
- Is it one page? Cut anything that doesn't help someone make the work.

## Extras Herbert may ask for

- **Concept starters**: 3 creative routes, each with a name, one-line idea, and a visual direction, since Herbert is a creative director.
- **Moodboard prompts**: 5 descriptive prompts for image search or AI image tools.
- **Shot list** for photo or video: scene, subject, framing, and notes.
