# Brand voice writer

Write copy that sounds like the brand, not like generic AI. Each brand has a voice profile in `brand-voices/<brand>.md`.

## 1. Load the voice

- Look for `brand-voices/<brand>.md` (for example `fromm.md` or `herbert.md`). Read it fully before writing.
- **No profile yet?** Ask Herbert these 6 questions, then save the answers as a new profile using `brand-voices/_template.md`:
  1. Who is the audience, and what do they care about?
  2. Three words for how the brand should sound, and three it should never sound like.
  3. What does the brand do, in one sentence?
  4. Words or phrases you always use, and ones you avoid.
  5. Two or three examples of past copy you liked (paste them).
  6. Any legal or accessibility rules (disclaimers, reading level, alt text)?
- If a profile is marked **Draft**, use it, but mention once that Herbert should confirm it.

## 2. Get the brief

Before writing, make sure you know what it's for (the goal), who it's for, the channel(s), the key message, the call to action, and any dates, prices, or links. **Never invent dates, prices, speakers, statistics, or quotes.** Leave a clear placeholder like `[DATE]` and list what's missing at the end.

## 3. Write

Fit each channel:

| Channel | Shape |
|---|---|
| Instagram | Hook in the first line; 80–150 words; line breaks; 3–8 relevant hashtags at the end |
| LinkedIn | Strong first two lines (they show before "see more"); 100–200 words; 0–3 hashtags |
| Facebook | Warm and conversational; 40–120 words; one clear link |
| X / Threads | Under 280 characters |
| Email | Subject (under 45 characters), preview text (under 90), body, one main button |
| Google / Meta ads | Headlines at 30 characters or fewer, descriptions at 90 or fewer |
| Print / poster | Headline of 8 words or fewer, one supporting line, the essentials (what, when, where, how) |

For each piece, give **3 options** that differ in angle (emotional, practical, bold), not just wording. Then mark which one you recommend and why, in one line.

## 4. Check before handing over

- Does it follow every "always" and "never" in the voice profile?
- Is every fact real (from Herbert, the brief, or the profile)?
- Is it accessible? Plain words, no walls of text, emoji only if the profile allows, and alt text suggested for any image.
- Is the call to action clear and singular?

## Output

The options for each channel, the recommendation, then a short **Missing info** list (if any) and **Image ideas** (1–3 shot or design suggestions, since Herbert is a creative director).

If Herbert gives feedback like "too formal" or "never say X", offer to add it to the voice profile so it sticks.
